Units & Chapters

Syllabus structure for JMA2501 Regulation 2023. Units are arranged in sequence with direct access to lecture notes and question banks.

UNIT I

Linear Programming

Weightage: 20%

Formulation of Linear Programming Problems (LPP), graphical method, simplex algorithm, artificial variable techniques (Big-M & Two-phase), unbounded solutions, and primal-to-dual problem formulation.

LPP Formulation Graphical Method Simplex Algorithm Big-M Method Duality Theory

UNIT II

Transportation and Assignment

Weightage: 20%

Transportation problem formulation, initial basic feasible solution (North-West Corner, Least Cost, and Vogel's Approximation Method), MODI test for optimality, and the Hungarian algorithm for assignment problems.

North-West Corner Least Cost Method Vogel's Approx (VAM) MODI Optimality Test Hungarian Assignment
View Notes Question Bank
UNIT III

Non-Linear Programming

Weightage: 20%

Unconstrained and constrained non-linear optimization, Hessian and Bordered Hessian matrix tests, Lagrangian multiplier methods for equality constraints, and Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions.

Unconstrained Extremum Hessian Matrix Bordered Hessian Lagrangian Multipliers Kuhn-Tucker (KKT)

UNIT IV

Dynamic Programming & Game Theory

Weightage: 20%

Bellman's Principle of Optimality, recursive stage-by-stage calculations, capital budgeting and cargo-loading models. Two-person zero-sum game theory, saddle points, mixed strategies, and dominance rules.

Bellman's Optimality Capital Budgeting Two-Person Zero-Sum Saddle Point Dominance Property

UNIT V

Queueing Models

Weightage: 20%

Characteristics of waiting lines, Kendall's notation, Little's equations, single-server systems (M/M/1):(∞/FIFO), finite queues (M/M/1):(K/FIFO), and multi-server systems (M/M/c):(∞/FIFO).

Kendall's Notation Little's Formulae Single Server (M/M/1) Finite Queue (M/M/1:K) Multi-Server (M/M/c)
